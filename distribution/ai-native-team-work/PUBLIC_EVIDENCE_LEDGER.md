## KPIs, achievements, and timelines

| ID | Claim | Status | Publication rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| K-01 | Time saved per workflow or per week. | needs approval | Require baseline, new value, measurement period, and what review time is included. |
| K-02 | Reduction in tools, handoffs, meetings, or manual steps. | needs approval | Require before and after counts plus the scope measured. |
| K-03 | Adoption rate or number of active users. | needs approval | Require denominator, time window, and definition of active. |
| K-04 | Output quality, acceptance rate, error rate, or escalation rate. | needs approval | Require a stable test and sample size. |
| K-05 | Cost, model spend, or cost per completed task. | needs approval | Require approved currency, range or exact figure, and measurement boundary. |
| K-06 | Build timeline for each stage. | needs approval | Require start and finish dates or an approved rounded duration. |
| K-07 | Number of workflows, agents, integrations, or automated actions. | needs approval | Require a dated inventory and definitions. |
| K-08 | Personal achievement such as managing multiple work streams with the system. | needs approval | Confirm the public framing and remove organization identities. |

Until a KPI is approved, the relevant post must teach the measurement method and use a blank template such as `baseline -> new value -> review cost -> measurement window`. It must not imply an achieved result.

## Approval checklist

Before changing `needs approval` to `approved`, record:

- the exact public wording;
- the evidence source;
- the measurement window where relevant;
- what was excluded from the measure;
- the date and user approval;
- whether the value should be exact, rounded, or expressed as a range.
