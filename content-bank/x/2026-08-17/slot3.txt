I found half my team using personal AI tools with company data. One for copy, one for code, one for summaries. All separate, all insecure.

My first instinct was to ban them.

Instead, we created a central 'recipe book.' If you find a great AI workflow, you document the prompt and the tool. We vet it for security, and then everyone can use it.

We went from dozens of solo tricks to one team playbook.
