Found our designer pasting UX copy into an AI tool nobody approved. Next day, a marketer was summarizing competitor pricing in a public chatbot.

The tempting move was a ban. Bans just push the same behavior onto personal laptops, where you see none of it.

We started a recipe book on the wiki instead. Find a workflow that saves you an hour, write down the prompt and the tool, we check nothing sensitive leaves, then anyone can use it.

Ten entries in a month. Half came from people I would never have guessed. The habits were already in the company, just hiding.
