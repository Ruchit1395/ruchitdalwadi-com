Mid-call last week, a developer asked if our new sprint board could show ticket priority. I said the PR was already up, I just wanted to finish the call first.

We moved sprints from Jira into boards built inside our own product. The board took a weekend. The feature request took less than a meeting.

Then I told the team something that landed strangely: priority will matter a lot less now. When the queue drains this fast, ranking it stops being the job. Deciding what belongs in it at all is the job.

Prioritization was a skill for managing scarcity. The scarcity is leaving.
