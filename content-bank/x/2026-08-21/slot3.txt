Mid-call last week, a developer asked if our sprint board could show priority. I said: the PR is already up. I just wanted to finish the call first.

We moved sprints from Jira into boards built inside our own product. The board took a weekend. The feature request took less than a meeting.

Then I told the team: priority will matter a lot less now. When the queue drains this fast, ranking it is not the job. Choosing what gets in is the job.

Prioritization was a skill for managing scarcity. The scarcity is leaving.
