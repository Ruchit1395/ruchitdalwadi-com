You've been rewriting system prompts for your agents, trying to squeeze out more performance. This week's lesson from the Claude Opus 5 launch: less is often more.

One team removed ~80% of their Claude Code system prompt for new models. What they learned:

1.  **Skills are the new system prompt**: Instead of long, complex instructions in the system prompt, define specific, reusable 'skills' for common tasks. This modularizes your agent's capabilities.
2.  **Claude.MDs for structured output**: For reliable structured output, use Claude.MD files. These act as templates, guiding the model to produce consistent formats without needing verbose prompt instructions.
3.  **Prompt injection resistance**: Shorter, clearer system prompts inherently reduce surface area for prompt injection attacks. Opus 5 is also reported to be their 'least prompt injectable model yet,' which helps even more.

Instead of trying to 'fix' the model with more words, focus on clear, atomic skills and structured output formats. Your agents will be more predictable and robust.
