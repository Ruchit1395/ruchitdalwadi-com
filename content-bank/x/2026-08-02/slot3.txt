You're still testing LLMs with "create an SVG of a pelican on a bicycle" prompts. That's yesterday's problem. 

Someone gave Opus 5 the first paragraph of Lord of the Rings and a 1M token budget. It went off for two hours and wrote 5500 lines of procedural JavaScript to render the story. 

It's janky, sure. But the fact an LLM can orchestrate and place elements in a complex, generative way for a narrative? That's a different league of capability. It means the model itself is building the world, not just a static image. 

Your eval harness needs to catch up. Test for emergent behavior in code, not just basic object generation.
