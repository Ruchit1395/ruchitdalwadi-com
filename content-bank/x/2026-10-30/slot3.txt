Our AI demo was slick. It understood every command we gave it. 'Show users from Texas.' Done. First real user in production typed, 'show me the good customers.' The system immediately threw a 500 error and crashed. We started a document, the Surprise Log, for every weird request that came in. That list of user mistakes is now our most valuable asset and the core of our real test suite. It turns out when users break your product, they're actually just writing the spec you missed.
