After 100,000 prompts and 15 million model messages, one operator found a critical shift: the newest AI models perform better with fewer rules and clearer boundaries. This means a different prompt stack is needed. Instead of trying to control every variable, focus on defining the core task and guardrails. This frees the model to leverage its capabilities without getting bogged down in excessive constraints. The shift isn't about more detail, it's about better-placed detail. Think less about micro-managing the output and more about macro-defining the intent. It's a counterintuitive move that can unlock significant performance gains.
