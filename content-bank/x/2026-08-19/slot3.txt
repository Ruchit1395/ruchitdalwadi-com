Our AI workout planner was a hit in beta. At the celebration lunch, finance slid a phone across the table: one morning of usage had cost more than the lunch, times fifty.

We had wired it to the biggest model available because that's what the prototype used. For turning a form into a workout plan, that's a fighter jet doing grocery runs.

A smaller fine-tuned model took two days to swap in. The plans got slightly blander, nobody said a word, and the bill stopped being a screenshot.

One boring afternoon before launch: find the cheapest model users won't notice.
