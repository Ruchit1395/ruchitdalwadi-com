Our new AI workout planner was a hit with beta users. It also generated a cloud bill larger than our payroll.

We used the most powerful model available because it was easy. It was also absurdly expensive.

We switched to a smaller, fine-tuned model. The quality dropped 10%, but the cost dropped 99%. A trade we should have made before launch.

Don't ship the demo model. Find the cheapest one that's good enough.
