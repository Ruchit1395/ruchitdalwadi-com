Before adding a planning layer to an automation, pull the logs from the last 50 runs and write down the step sequence for each one.

All 50 identical: that is a workflow. Hardcode the sequence, keep one model call inside it, and it becomes debuggable. The planner was latency, cost, and nondeterminism with no job.

Sequences genuinely diverge based on what earlier steps found: that is agent territory. Research across messy sources. Triage on ambiguous input. Paths that cannot be enumerated upfront.

The test takes five minutes and settles an argument that otherwise runs for weeks. The boring version ships all week without surprises, and nobody gets paged for a planner improvising on a Tuesday.
