The distance between an AI demo and an AI product is a boring checklist, not a smarter model.

What the demo skipped: retries with backoff. Timeouts. A fallback when the model returns garbage. Input validation before the call. Output validation after it. Rate limit handling. A log of every request and response. A way to replay yesterday's failure. Cost tracking per feature. An owner for the eval set.

None of this is intellectually hard. All of it is unglamorous, which is why the six-month gap between working demo and trustworthy product keeps surprising teams that expected the model to be the hard part.

Budget the checklist, not just the model. The checklist is where the schedule lives.
