On a team call last week, a developer asked if our new sprint board could show ticket priority.

Quick background. We ran sprints in Jira for years. Then we built sprint boards inside our own product and moved the team over. Partly to feel what our customers feel. Partly because the build took a weekend now, not a quarter.

So he asks about priority. And I heard myself say: the PR is already up. I just wanted to finish this call first.

A feature request, closed before the meeting ended.

Not because I am fast. Because the gap between wanting a feature and having it collapsed this year.

Then I told the team something that landed strange. Priority will matter a lot less now. When the queue drains this fast, ranking the queue is not the job anymore. Choosing what gets in the queue is the job.

We spent ten years getting good at prioritization. It was a skill for managing scarcity.

The scarcity is leaving.
