A CEO I know saw some slick demo online and got an idea. He went to his team and said he wanted an AI to write their product roadmap. Just dump in the backlog and all the customer interviews, that was the whole brief.

So they spent a whole quarter on it, got the office GPU fans screaming. And the thing looked incredible. It could slurp up thousands of data points and spit out a roadmap that was perfectly formatted and looked totally plausible.

They rolled it out internally to the product team. Crickets. I don't think anyone used it more than once.

The team figured out that building a roadmap isn't a summarization task. It's a negotiation. It's about the arguments, the painful trade offs, and the sheer effort of getting a room full of smart people to point in the same direction. The AI was great at generating a document, but it couldn't create genuine alignment.
