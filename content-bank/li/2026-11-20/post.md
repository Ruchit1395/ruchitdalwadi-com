Our lead designer started playing with an AI image tool for mockups, sitting at his desk by the window. Suddenly he was churning out work at a speed we'd never seen, and the quality was just incredible. Management saw this and immediately wanted to scale it up across the whole design team. So we bought ten licenses for everyone. It was a total mess. No one could get anything close to his results. It turned out his secret wasn't a documented workflow. It was a thousand tiny habits, this whole chain of specific prompts and weird editing techniques he couldn't even explain himself. We realized you can't just buy a license for an expert's intuition. A real team process is something you can write down and give to a new hire.
