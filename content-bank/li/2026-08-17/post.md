I walked past our lead designer's desk and saw her using a third-party AI tool to generate UX copy. A day later, I saw a junior marketer pasting competitor data into a public chatbot to get it summarized.

Everyone was using AI. But it was all personal, disconnected, and insecure. We had dozens of private workflows and zero shared knowledge.

We didn't ban the tools. Instead, we started a 'recipe book' in our wiki. Anyone who found a useful AI workflow had to document the prompt, the tool, and the result. We reviewed them for security and effectiveness.

Now, instead of 30 people fumbling in the dark, we have a shared, vetted library of 10 powerful workflows everyone can use. We turned individual tricks into team leverage.
