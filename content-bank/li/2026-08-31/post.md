I remember so many meetings in that stuffy room on the third floor, sitting around a table with the planning poker cards, arguing about whether some ticket was a 3 or a 5. It was a whole ceremony. Last month, we just turned story points off entirely. Our new system is that if a ticket is too big for the AI to build in one pass, it’s just too big. You have to break it down. So now we just count the number of tickets we finish. We even switched to one week sprints, and honestly a single week now feels more productive than two weeks used to.
