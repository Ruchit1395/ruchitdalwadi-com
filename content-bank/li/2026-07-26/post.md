I've been watching the conversation around Jack Dorsey's 'Buzz' project, and one idea stands out: shared compute. 

We're past the point where open models are significantly behind proprietary ones. The real bottleneck for many builders is the hardware. Running powerful models often requires expensive GPUs and a non-trivial setup process. 

Shared compute flips this. One person runs the machine, loads an open model, and others can access it. This isn't just about cost savings; it's about making advanced AI accessible to a much broader audience. For founders and operators, this means less friction to experiment and deploy, potentially accelerating product cycles and reducing infrastructure overhead. It's a pragmatic solution to a very real problem.
