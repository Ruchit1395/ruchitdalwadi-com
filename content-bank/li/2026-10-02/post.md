A CEO sees a competitor's AI video demo. He forwards the link to his head of product with a simple note. We need this. Two week sprint.

The team watches the video, which had a weirdly cinematic background track. They know it’s mostly faked and the real thing would take at least six months. But saying no outright is a good way to get labeled as difficult. So they didn't.

Instead, they spent three days building a tiny version that actually worked. It did maybe a fraction of what the competitor’s demo promised, but it was real. They showed it to the CEO. This is what's possible this week. We think we can add another small piece every ten days or so.

The whole dynamic in the room shifted. Expectations were reset, and the impossible deadline was forgotten.
