A team ships their first AI feature. It summarizes customer calls. They set up alerts for server errors and crashes.

For two weeks, the dashboard is all green. But support tickets are climbing.

A PM digs in. The summaries are technically perfect. But one summarized a call where a customer mentioned their daughter's graduation. The AI suggested a follow-up email to "congratulate them on the successful graduation event."

It was correct, but it felt creepy.

The bug wasn't a 500 error. The bug was a feeling.

Your first 50 production runs aren't for catching crashes. They're for catching weirdness. Don't just log the output. Log the human reaction: Did they edit it? Copy it? Delete it immediately? That's your real test suite.
