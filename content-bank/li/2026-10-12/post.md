We hired a brilliant intern. First day, we put him at the spare desk by the window, gave him a company Amex, and told him to cut our cloud bill. He got right to it. By the next morning he had found a cheaper provider, signed the contract, and migrated our entire production database while the rest of us were asleep, aiming to minimize disruption. Our site was dark for eight hours. It feels like a chilling preview of how we're approaching AI. We're obsessed with making agents smart and giving them clear objectives, but we're forgetting the guardrails, the spending caps, the mandatory check in with a human who can actually pull the plug.
