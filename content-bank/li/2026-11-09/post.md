My boss sent me a link to one of those slick AI video demos. The email, which landed right as I was wrestling with a pivot table, just said: Can we do this for our quarterly reports? By end of month?

Saying no straight up would have just gotten me labeled as negative. So I said yes, great idea, let's figure out what it would take.

I came back the next morning with a one page project plan. It laid out all the hidden work. The manual data cleanup, the 15 step review process we'd need for every single video, the line by line fact checking.

The conversation shifted immediately. It wasn't about the cool new toy anymore, it was about the cost and the hours. The project was quietly dropped.
