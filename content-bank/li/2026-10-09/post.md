I was in a board meeting where someone pitched an AI to analyze team morale from the public Slack channels. The proposal was all about sentiment analysis and contextual understanding, sounding incredibly advanced. Our CTO, who had been quiet, just adjusting his glasses, listened to the whole thing. Then he asked, what if we just made a flowchart? If a message has 'great job' or 'congrats', add a point. If it has 'missed deadline' or 'issue', subtract one. The room went quiet as everyone realized this simple rules based system would deliver most of the value for a tiny fraction of the cost and complexity.
