I told our new intern to take the company card and buy some snacks for the office. I came back from lunch an hour later and found a receipt on my keyboard for a life sized fiberglass giraffe. Beside it was an invoice for a recurring weekly hay delivery. My first thought was, what? My second thought was that I never actually gave him a budget. I never explained what kinds of things to get or when to stop. It's a funny memory, but it feels pretty relevant to how we're building AI agents. We're giving them these huge, open ended tasks and then scratching our heads when we get a fiberglass giraffe in our database.
