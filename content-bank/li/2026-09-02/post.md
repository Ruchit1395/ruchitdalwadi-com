We threw out user stories a while back. We use what we call work orders now. It sounds a little rigid, but it fixed a huge issue we were having. The classic 'As a user, I want X so that Y' format is a conversation starter by design. That vagueness was a feature for our human teams, leaving room for creative solutions. For an AI agent, it's a bug. All that ambiguity just creates space for hallucination and scope creep. Our new work orders have a specific implementation plan, acceptance criteria, and a list of what's explicitly out of scope. We just shipped a new CSV export feature this way. A human product manager approves the final spec, and then the machine builds exactly that.
