Our new AI powered report generator looked great in staging, everyone was excited, so we shipped it to our top five customers.

The first person to try it was a CFO. With a single click, right from his standing desk, he decided to run a query on his company's entire annual sales history.

Not even ten minutes later, the cloud bill alert went off. That one query cost us $400.

We then had the fun job of sending an email asking him to please stop using the feature we had just proudly announced.
