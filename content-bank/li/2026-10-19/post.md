A team I know shipped a feature to auto generate quarterly reports. You gave it a client's name, it pulled their data from the warehouse, and wrote the whole thing. Technically, it was a huge achievement. A month later they checked the metrics and saw a ghost town. Nobody was touching it.

They finally sat down with a few users. One of them, Brenda, pointed at the button during the meeting and said she was terrified of it. What if it pulled the wrong number and sent it straight to her biggest client, the one with the office in downtown Chicago? She’d be fired on the spot. They had built a powerful tool, but she needed to be in control.

The fix had nothing to do with the model. They just changed the workflow. Now, the AI drafts the report and then hands it over for a person to review and actually send. That boring little approval step is what made the whole thing take off.
