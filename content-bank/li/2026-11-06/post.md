A startup I know shipped a free AI writing assistant for students. The launch went well, maybe too well. A few kids at a big university found it, told their friends, and suddenly it felt like it was writing every term paper on campus. The team was celebrating, eating pizza and watching the user growth chart go vertical. Then the first monthly API bill arrived. It was more than their entire office rent. The thing about AI unit economics is they aren't like normal software. Every query costs you real money. Free can bankrupt you fast. You have to model your cost per active user before you go live. For every free feature, you better know your circuit breaker, the point where you either start charging, rate limiting, or find a cheaper model.
