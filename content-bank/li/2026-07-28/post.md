The conversation around AI agents often defaults to coding or devops, but the real silent revolution is happening in marketing automation.

Imagine an AI agent managing your entire Facebook ad account. It's not just scheduling posts; it's a full-cycle operator:

1.  **Research**: Scrapes platforms like Reddit for genuine customer pain points and emerging trends.
2.  **Creative Generation**: Produces on-brand ad copy and visuals, tailored to those insights.
3.  **Campaign Management**: Publishes ads, monitors performance in real-time.
4.  **Optimization**: Automatically kills underperforming ads, scales up winners, and iterates on what's working.

This is code in the cloud, making decisions on a loop, directly tied to live business data. It researches, acts, reads results, improves, and goes again.

The shift from human-in-the-loop to agent-in-the-loop for marketing spend is a massive lever for startups and established businesses alike. The earliest movers are already building these autonomous loops that directly drive revenue.

If you're building in AI, look at areas where agents can take over entire, measurable business processes. Marketing, with its clear metrics and iterative nature, is ripe for this transformation. The stack is accessible, involving tools like Perplexity for initial data scraping.
