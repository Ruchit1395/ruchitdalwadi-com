Last year we opened a req for a 'Prompt Engineer.' All the resumes were a sea of jargon, just walls of text about chain of thought and retrieval augmented generation. The interviews were rough. We went through ten candidates, and I remember the whiteboard marker I was using was running out of ink by the end. Not a single person asked who the user was. Nobody asked what problem we were actually solving. We closed the job. A week later we posted a new one: 'Product Manager, AI Features.' The main requirement was the ability to write a clear one page spec. We hired the very first person we interviewed for that role. She had a background as a technical writer.
