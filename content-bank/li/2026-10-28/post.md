We had a job posting up for a Prompt Engineer. The first person I interviewed was really focused on crafting the perfect sentence, on understanding the model's personality. Then the next candidate came in and she barely mentioned the prompt at all. She wanted to see our customer support tickets. She asked which dashboards our execs look at every morning, and what was the most mind numbing task our data analysts were stuck with. She carried a small, worn notebook with a pen clipped to the spiral binding, and it was full of diagrams of user workflows. We hired her. We even ended up changing the job title to Product Engineer.
