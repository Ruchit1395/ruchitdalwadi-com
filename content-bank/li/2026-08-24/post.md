A friend of mine bought a decommissioned fire truck. It's an old Pierce Saber, an incredible piece of engineering, and the sound it makes when he starts it up is something else. The thing is, he lives in a one bedroom apartment. He has nowhere to park it and, thankfully, no fires to put out. He just likes the truck. This is what so many conversations about AI strategy feel like in 2024. Every product team seems to have a shiny fire truck sitting in its roadmap. We get so fascinated with the impressive tool that we lose track of the problem it's meant to solve. There's a lot of focus on whether we can build it, and not nearly enough on whether we have a fire that actually needs putting out.
