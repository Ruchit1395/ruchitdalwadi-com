I was walking over to the coffee machine when I heard two of our PMs in a full blown debate about the prompt for an AI feature that generates user personas. One was convinced his complex XML format was the only way. The other was just as certain her simple 10 line system prompt was superior.

I had them pull up the outputs side by side. You could barely tell the difference.

So we made a rule. If you want to change a prompt, you need to prove in a blind test that it improves the output by at least 10 percent. The arguments pretty much vanished.
