One of our junior engineers, a really sharp guy, came to me after struggling for a week with a prompt. He was trying to get an LLM to summarize some dense vendor contracts. He showed me the prompt and it was a monster, maybe 400 words long, full of rules and instructions and examples of what not to do. The outputs were a total coin toss. Sometimes it worked, sometimes it hallucinated entire clauses. I read it over, took his keyboard, selected everything in the little text box, and deleted it. Then I pulled up a summary one of our actual lawyers had written for a similar contract. I just pasted the text of that summary into the prompt window. Nothing else. The first one it generated was perfect. It hasn't missed one since.
