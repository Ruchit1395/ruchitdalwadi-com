One of our PMs was completely stuck trying to get an AI model to summarize articles without mentioning our competitors. He spent the better part of a week just wrestling with the prompt, throwing in all kinds of negative constraints and examples. He was about to scrap the whole feature.

An engineer on the team came over to his desk, which was covered in sticky notes. She listened for a minute, then bypassed the prompt entirely. She wrote a quick pre processing script that just stripped the competitor names out of the source text.

The whole thing was working perfectly twenty minutes later.
