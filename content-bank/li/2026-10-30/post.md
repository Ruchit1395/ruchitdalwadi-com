We thought our demo was perfect. The AI could take any natural language request, like 'Show me all users in California who signed up in May,' and turn it into a flawless database query. It worked every single time. Then came the first day in production. A ticket landed at 9:15 AM from a user who had typed, 'show me the numbers from last week, but not the bad ones.' The whole system just fell over. My first instinct was to call it a bad user request, but then it hit me that this was the entire point of the tool. We started a simple document we called the Surprise Log and pasted in every weird, ambiguous, or broken request we saw. That log is now the absolute core of our test suite. It's proven more valuable than any formal spec we ever wrote, because it showed us what the real requirements were all along.
