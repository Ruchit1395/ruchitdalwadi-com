We put one of those fancy Jura espresso machines in the office kitchen, free for everyone. For the first month it was great. Just three of us really used it, and the bill for the artisan coffee beans was completely fine. Then the sales team discovered it. They started making lattes before every single call. The next invoice I saw from our supplier was for four figures. Our small perk had become a pretty big line item. This is the exact same trajectory we just saw with our first internal AI tool. It was a simple summarizer. Engineering loved it and the costs were basically zero. Then sales found it and started running every client email they had through it. It’s a strange lesson to learn that your best case adoption scenario can be your worst case cost scenario. It pays to model the price of wild success before you ship, not after you get a shocking bill.
