The board meeting wrapped. As I was leaving, the room smelling of stale coffee and whiteboard markers, my CEO grabbed me. He was practically bouncing, asking if we could use our AI to predict next quarter's sales, even find our next ten biggest customers. My first instinct was to launch into a whole thing about data readiness. Instead I just said I’d try something. An hour later I called him over to my desk to see a prototype. The top predicted new customer was a billion dollar firm called Cosmic Innovations that sold rocket parts out of a tiny town in Wyoming. A company which, of course, did not exist. He started laughing. He got it instantly. So the project changed. We're not predicting the future anymore, we're building a classification tool for our inbound leads so sales knows who to call first.
