At a past company, the internal portal's login page took a full twelve seconds to load because of some ancient backend service. People had to log into this thing multiple times a day. It had this little spinning icon that wasn't even centered on the screen. Instead of fixing the lag, leadership got excited about AI and held a hackathon. The winning project? An AI that generated a new motivational quote for the login screen every morning. It got deployed. So now, while you waited, you got to read things like, "Today is a great day to achieve your potential." No one ever mentioned the quotes in meetings, but you still heard people muttering about the load times in the kitchen.
