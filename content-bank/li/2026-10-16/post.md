We had a junior support hire who was really struggling, just couldn't keep up with the ticket queue. We tried everything for him, new scripts and phrasing guides, but nothing was clicking.

He was on the verge of quitting. As a total Hail Mary, sitting at my desk with a lukewarm coffee, I gave him read only access to our Confluence, the internal engineering wiki. It's a chaotic mess of old bug reports and half finished product specs.

Overnight, he became our best agent.

It makes me think about how we're all trying to improve AI with better prompts. We spend ages polishing the question, when maybe what the model really needs is just the key to the library.
