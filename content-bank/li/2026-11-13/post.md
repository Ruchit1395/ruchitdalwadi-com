A team I know was demoing a new AI assistant to the sales group. For any customer question, it could write and run a fresh database query, generate a chart on the fly, and draft a perfect email reply in seconds. The whole demo was running off a staging server in the corner. The VP of Sales was ecstatic. Ship it. Ship it now. The first bill came in a month later. That one feature's cost was more than a senior engineer's annual salary. We learned that every single one of those answers was chaining together a dozen expensive model calls. Everyone had calculated the cost to build it, but no one had asked what it cost to run it.
